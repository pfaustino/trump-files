    Read this first

    • Not every item names Trump personally. Detention, deportation, shooting, and strike cards name ICE, DHS, the Pentagon, or officials. Each card says who is named.
    • Allegations are not findings. Trump denies wrongdoing in every matter here. Only the cases marked convicted or liable reflect a legal finding, and those are still on appeal.
    • Presumption of innocence. Criminal charges are accusations until proven beyond a reasonable doubt.
    • Stakes ratings are editorial. They estimate how severe the consequence would be if proven, from 1 (low) to 5 (removal from office or prison).
    • Status may lag. Statuses were checked against news and court sources on Nov 11, 2026. Confirm against the court docket before relying on any item.
    • Not legal advice. This is an educational overview. For real decisions, talk to a licensed attorney.
